Ready to quit the willpower-based approach to digital detox?

TATSU is a new kind of passive digital detox app built on one simple idea: "no willpower needed." You don't have to force yourself to put down your phone. Just let the system take over — it smartly and stress-free reduces your daily screen time.


💡 What makes TATSU special

1. "Passive" — so it actually sticks
Unlike timer-based screen lock apps that rely on your initiative, TATSU automatically and smartly interrupts app launches based on your settings. A gentle nudge right when you think "maybe I'll check my phone" blocks those mindless social media scrolls and video binges at the root. You literally don't have to do anything.

2. Collect nostalgic "Pixel Cats"
Your detox efforts earn you adorable pixel-art "Pixel Cats." Different cats appear based on your screen-free time and completed challenges. Every cat you discover is recorded in the Cat Encyclopedia, turning the joy of collecting into long-term motivation for your detox journey. (More species coming in future updates!)

3. Absolute privacy protection
TATSU is meant to be your strongest ally. All your data — settings, usage logs, collected cats — is stored entirely as local data on your device. Nothing is ever sent to external servers. Detox with complete peace of mind.

4. Beautiful, intuitive analytics
Which apps did you restrict, and by how much? How did your week go? Your progress is visualized on a modern, sleek UI. Seeing daily results transforms "not using your phone" into a genuinely positive experience.
